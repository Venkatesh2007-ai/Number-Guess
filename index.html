How To Play NumberQuest

🎯
Objective

Guess the secret randomly generated number within the permitted attempts.

🌡️
Hot / Cold Gauge

The proximity thermometer measures how close you are. "Boiling" means you're within inches of the answer!

🔍
Dynamic Search Window

Every time you guess, the game automatically narrows down the lowest and highest boundary values.

💡
Detective Hints

Need a boost? Spend 1 attempt to unlock hints about whether the number is even, odd, or divisible by key factors.
